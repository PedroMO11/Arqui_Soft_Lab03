# Juan Rodríguez — Recipient

## Role
Individual who receives a remittance sent by a family member or friend from another
country. Collects the funds either in cash at a SendIt branch or via bank deposit.

## A day in their work
- Juan is notified that a remittance is available for him, along with the tracking code.
- If the payout channel is cash: he goes to a SendIt branch, presents ID and the tracking
  code; the agent validates his identity against the sender's data and hands him the
  cash.
- If the payout channel is bank deposit: SendIt validates his identity against the KYC
  provider and sends the funds directly to his account — no branch visit required.
- If he does not collect within the allowed window, the remittance expires and the funds
  are retained by SendIt — neither refunded to the sender nor paid to him.

## Needs
- Needs to know the status of the money transfer a family member or friend sent him.
- Needs to check that status using only the tracking code, without having to create an
  account in the system first.
- Wants the money to be available to withdraw as soon as possible.
- Needs to know exactly how many days he has to collect before the remittance expires, so
  he doesn't lose the money.

## Current pains
- "It takes too long for the money to reach me."
- "I'm afraid someone else could impersonate me and collect in my place."

## What the system gives them
- Real-time visibility of the remittance status and its tracking code, lookup-able with
  no account or registration.
- Identity verification (at the counter, or remotely for deposits) that blocks
  impersonation.
- Clear, upfront visibility of the collection deadline before the remittance expires.
